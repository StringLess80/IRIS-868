# Changelog

## [2.0.0] - 2026-11-05 - Rev B
Not bring-up tested.

### Added
- J5 and J7 edge headers, W25Q128JV flash, PC13 button, VBUS monitor
- NRST on the SWD header
- R13 BOOT0 pull-down

### Changed
- Radio from E220-900M22S (LLCC68) to E22-900M22S (SX1262)
- ANT rerouted out from under IC1, J6 moved
- C8 and C10 moved closer to their pins
- USB D+ pull-up driven by PB8
- Board size 78.8 x 36.6 mm, green mask, white silkscreen

### Removed
- Test pad under IC1

## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-15 - Rev A
First hardware revision.
